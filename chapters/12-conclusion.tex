\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusion}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The thesis addresses the gap between an elegant conditioned-movement model and the heterogeneous data, preprocessing, interfaces, diagnostics, and outputs required by real animal-movement studies.
  \item Its central contribution is the end-to-end MoveApps workflow from environmental annotation and projection through segmentation, kernel and terrain-weight construction to movement reconstruction and spatial summaries.
  \item Conditioned random walks provide the computational core, but the scientific result depends on the validity and traceability of every surrounding stage.
  \item Insert one evidence-backed answer for each research question after the final experiment manifest is frozen.
  \item Close with the practical implication: ecologists can assemble, inspect, and compare realistic movement reconstructions without treating the theory as a self-contained black box.
\end{itemize}
